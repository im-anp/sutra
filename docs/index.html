Features

Knows your context. Does the work. Asks first.

Sunday ties together your notes, decisions, plans, inbox and learning — and remembers, so you don't have to.

Memory you own

Everything Sunday learns about you lives in plain markdown files in a folder on your disk. Open them in Obsidian, edit them, back them up however you like.

  • Before every reply, it recalls the notes that matter to what you just said.
  • Search runs locally, with on-device embeddings.
  • Delete the folder and it's gone. No cloud copy.

How it works

Up and running in two minutes.

No account to create and no server to run. One command installs it; one key powers every model.

  1. 1

    Install

    Paste the install command into Terminal. It downloads Sunday and adds the sunday command.

  2. 2

    Add your key

    Paste an OpenRouter key when asked and choose where your memory folder lives. That's the whole setup.

  3. 3

    Talk to it

    Run sunday and it opens in your browser. Update any time with sunday update.

Privacy

Yours, and only yours.

Sunday is built for one person on one computer. Your notes, email and logins never leave your Mac — only the model calls go out, with your own key.

  • Local by designRuns on your Mac and only answers your Mac. Nothing listens on your network.
  • Plain files you ownMemory is markdown in a folder you choose. Delete it and it's gone.
  • Your key, your modelsBring an OpenRouter key and pick the fast and strong models in Settings.
  • Remote is opt-inOff until you set it up. Your Mac reaches out to the relay over HTTPS, messages are held at most 10 minutes, and sunday remote revoke unpairs any browser.
  • No accountThere's no sign-in: whoever uses this computer is treated as you, so keep it yours.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Sunday free?

Yes. Sunday itself is free. It uses AI models through your own OpenRouter key, so you pay OpenRouter only for what you use.

Does my data leave my Mac?

Your notes, files, email and logins stay on your Mac. Only the model calls go out, with your own key. Remote access is off until you set it up.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), with Node.js 24 or newer installed.

Do I need Obsidian?

No. Sunday's memory is a folder of markdown files. Obsidian is a nice way to browse and edit them, but it's optional.

Can I use it from my phone?

Yes. Run sunday remote setup on your Mac and pair your phone at app.sundaybot.me, or pair Telegram. Sunday keeps running on your Mac.

Is Sunday the same as Sutra?

Yes. Sutra was renamed to Sunday in version 0.1.8. Your data carries over, and the sutra command still works.
